The address is checked first: it must answer with a listings list whose items have listing_id, full_type and price. The market job then reads every page each hour; prices are per item, and only items in the library get a price. Everyone can pick an enabled marketplace in the editor. Ignore overpriced leaves listings out of the prices (they stay recorded): at or above a placeholder price ("not for sale"), or over that many times the item's median price (when it has at least 3 listings and the price is at least 100). Use 0 to turn that rule off. Server settings are the game server's Server Economy sandbox settings: everyone who picks the marketplace gets that server's ATM, store and Black Market prices.
